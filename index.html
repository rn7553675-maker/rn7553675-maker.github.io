Help for grown-ups

How do I play?
Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump and swipe down to roll. A short tutorial runs the first time.
Where are the settings and the "Remove Ads" button?
Tap the gear on the home screen and answer the grown-up question. The "Parents" button in the settings opens Remove Ads, Restore purchase, the privacy information and the credits.
I bought "Remove Ads" but I still see ads / I changed phones.
Open Parents and tap "Restore Purchase". Use the same Google account that made the purchase. Refunds are handled by Google Play: see Google Play refund help.
Does the game work offline?
Yes. Everything works offline except the online ranks and ads. Scores are sent the next time the device is online.
What are the online ranks?
Lists of the highest scores of today and of all time. Each player gets a fun name made from preset words, like "Happy Monkey 482". Children can pick other preset words but cannot type names, and there is no chat.
The game runs slowly on our tablet.
Open the settings and choose a lower quality level, or leave it on "Auto" so the game picks one.

Delete our online ranks data

E-mail 8birdgame@gmail.com with the subject "Banana Dash data deletion" and the ranks name shown on the purple name button of the Ranks screen. If you can, add the score and the day it was played. We delete the scores and ranks name within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Everything else is stored only on the device: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings to delete it.

Contact

Questions, ideas or a problem? Write to 8birdgame@gmail.com. Please mention your device model and Android version if something is not working.
