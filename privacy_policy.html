Banana Dash: Monkey Run
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · App package: com.bananadash.game

The short version

  • Banana Dash is a game for children and their families. We do not collect personal information from children.
  • No accounts to create, no log-in, no chat, no typing of names or messages, no photos, no microphone, no location permission and no contacts.
  • Game progress is saved on the device.
  • For the online ranks, the game signs in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services. Only a random player ID, a fun name made from preset words and scores are stored.
  • Ads come from Google AdMob only. They are marked as child-directed, rated G and non-personalized. The game does not use the Android advertising ID.
  • The optional "Remove Ads" purchase and the settings are behind a grown-up question.
  • Parents can ask us to delete the online ranks data at any time: 8birdgame@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Banana Dash: Monkey Run ("Banana Dash", "the game") is made by the Banana Dash developer ("we", "us"). You can contact us at 8birdgame@gmail.com.

2. Children's privacy

Banana Dash is designed for children under 13 and their families. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, including its rules for children) and the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Every online service in the game is set up for child users, as described below. If you believe a child has given us personal information (for example by e-mail), please contact us and we will delete it.

3. Information that stays on the device

  • Game progress and settings (bananas, outfits, pets, stickers, missions, records, sound and quality settings) are saved in the app's private storage on the device.
  • Error log. If something goes wrong, the game writes a short technical error log to a file on the device to help fix problems. It contains no personal information and the game never sends it anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this.

4. What the game does not do

  • No account creation, no sign-in screen and no passwords.
  • No chat, messages, friend lists or free text. Children cannot type anything.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, files or contacts.
  • No location permission (no GPS).
  • No sharing to social networks and no links that take children out of the game.
  • No Android advertising ID: the game does not request the AD_ID permission.
  • We never sell or rent information.

5. Online ranks – Unity Gaming Services

The "Ranks" screen shows the top runs of today and of all time. It uses Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards by Unity Technologies.

  • When the device is online, the game signs in silently and anonymously. This creates a random player ID. No name, e-mail, phone number or other personal detail is asked for or sent.
  • What is stored online: the random player ID, a playful ranks name made from preset words (for example "Happy Monkey 482"), and the game's scores. Children can only pick other preset words; they cannot type a name.
  • Other players see only the preset ranks name and the score. Nobody can send messages.
  • To run the service, Unity also processes technical information such as the IP address, device type and diagnostic information about the service.
  • The "Today" list starts fresh every day at 00:00 UTC. The "All Time" list is kept until we delete the entry (see section 11) or the game is retired.
  • Without internet the game works normally; scores are sent later.

6. Advertising – Google AdMob

The game shows ads from Google AdMob only. No other ad networks and no mediation are used. Every ad request is set up as follows:

  • Child-directed treatment ("tag for child-directed treatment", COPPA) and under the age of consent.
  • Maximum ad content rating: G (suitable for all audiences).
  • Non-personalized ads only: no interest-based ads and no remarketing.
  • No banner ads. Short full-screen ads may appear only between runs (never when the game starts), and a child can choose to watch an optional video, marked with a video icon, for bonus bananas.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address (which gives an approximate location), the app set ID, device and app information, interactions with ads and diagnostic information. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · AdMob child-directed treatment.

7. Purchases

"Remove Ads" is an optional one-time purchase behind a grown-up question. Payment is handled by Google Play; we never receive payment card details. Unity In-App Purchasing processes the purchase receipt and technical information so that the purchase can be checked and restored on a new device.

8. The Unity engine

Banana Dash is made with the Unity engine. Unity may receive anonymous technical information about the device (for example model, operating system and graphics chip) and about engine errors, to keep the engine working well.

9. Parental gate

The settings and the "Parents" area (Remove Ads, Restore purchase, this privacy information and the credits) open only after answering a grown-up question (a multiplication written in words).

11. Parents' choices and data deletion

  • Delete online ranks data: e-mail 8birdgame@gmail.com with the subject "Banana Dash data deletion". Please include the ranks name shown on the purple name button of the Ranks screen (for example "Happy Monkey 482") and, if you can, the score and the day it was played. You do not need to send any other personal information. We delete the player's scores and ranks name within 30 days, confirm by e-mail and then delete your e-mail.
  • Delete everything on the device: uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Play without online services: turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data. The game works offline; ranks and ads need internet.
  • Remove ads: buy "Remove Ads" in the Parents area.
  • Parents can also ask us what information we hold and ask us to stop processing it.

12. Security and storage

All information sent by the game travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data on the device is kept in the app's private storage. Our service providers may store information on servers in other countries, protected by their own safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game's release notes.
